Privacy

This site is run by Michael Elkær Meisner, Denmark, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions can be sent to me on LinkedIn.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies. Nothing is stored on your device unless you choose to be left out of the statistics.
  • It uses no advertising tools and does not follow you across other sites.
  • Its fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google or other font services.

Visitor statistics

To see whether the site and the book are being read, I count page views, file downloads and clicks on links to other sites with Plausible Analytics, a European statistics service. When you open a page, your browser loads a small script from plausible.io and sends the page address, the site you came from, your browser, your operating system and your device type. It also sends how far down the page you scrolled and how long the page was open.

Plausible uses your IP address to work out your approximate location and to count you as one visitor for that day. It does not store the IP address, and a visit on one day cannot be linked to a visit on another. What I see is totals per page, file and link, and I cannot identify you from them. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is used.

If you do not want your visits counted, you can leave this browser out of the statistics. That choice is saved as a single setting in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Plausible Insights OÜ in Estonia runs the service as my data processor under a data processing agreement. The data is kept on servers in Germany and Finland and does not leave the EU. Plausible's data policy describes the details.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you visit. GitHub's own privacy statement describes how it handles that data.

Contact through LinkedIn

If you contact me on LinkedIn, I use what you write only to answer you. LinkedIn handles the message under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data about you. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
