Make the Regime yours

The Regime is not a system and not a department. It is a small set of things an organisation does consistently. This guide helps you decide how it fits yours.

Ten questions about your organisation, a checklist of decisions for each of the seven tools, and a plan on one page. It takes fifteen to twenty minutes. The guide says what to decide and in what order. The book and its Appendix A say why and how. Your answers stay on this page and are gone when you close it. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.

Decisions for each tool
